# Changelog

## 2.0.0

omnibase/drive: a shared drive for an omnibase site - folders and files shared by audience and group, tags, downloads counted, retention - on omnisense/omnibase 7.0's agnostic repositories (the finder language), any persistence behind them: the download counters are counted, moved and deleted with updateBy(), Change and deleteBy(), each query written once and compiled by omnibridge/doctrine or omnibridge/eloquent; writes go through the unit of work.
